⭐ Line Games — Rules

Setting up (everyone, once)

  1. On iPhone, tap Share then Add to Home Screen BEFORE running Setup. The home-screen app keeps its own saved data.
  2. Everyone opens the app and updates together, so all phones run the same version.
  3. Pick contiguous player numbers 1..N (no gaps), and enter the same N and the same starting round code on every phone.
  4. Each round shows three emojis, the sync check. One person reads them aloud: "everyone see 🍩🚀🎈?" If anyone differs, fix the code, N or settings until they match.
  5. Tap Next round together. If someone leaves, renumber everyone 1..N and set the new N.
  6. At the end of a round, one person taps Show answer.

🕵️ Imposter

  • Everyone holds to see the same secret word; the imposter sees none.
  • Go around giving one-word clues. Do not be too obvious.
  • Vote on the imposter. One person taps Show answer.

🗺️ Spyfall

  • Everyone sees the place and a role; the spy sees only the list of places.
  • Take turns asking each other questions about the place.
  • Vote out the spy, or the spy guesses the place. Timer is 8:00.

🎯 Bingo

  • Each player has their own card. Tap a square when you spot it.
  • Five in a row, column or diagonal wins. The center is free.
  • Spot quietly: never point at, photograph or comment on strangers.

📡 Wavelength

  • The clue-giver secretly sees the target spot between two extremes.
  • They give a clue; everyone else slides to where they think the target is.
  • Reveal shows the target and the points (closer scores more).

🎭 Secret Missions

  • Each player gets a different secret mission for the day.
  • Complete it without being noticed. Reveal at dinner and tick success.

🧠 Trivia Showdown

  • Each player picks Kid, Teen or Adult. Ten questions, four choices.
  • Lock your answer, then everyone taps Reveal together.
  • The end screen shows "Score x/10". Compare out loud.

🎢 Ride Trivia

  • Everyone picks the same ride (read it aloud; the sync check confirms it).
  • Answer its questions with Lock, Reveal together, Next. Compare scores.

🤥 Two Truths & a Lie

  • Three statements: two true, one lie. Pick the lie and lock your vote.
  • Reveal together to see the lie and why. Your tally stays on your phone.

⚖️ Over / Under

  • A prompt and a reference number. Lock Over or Under (add a number if you like).
  • Check the real answer in the park, then tap "I was right" or "I was wrong".

Numbers must be contiguous 1..N. Not affiliated with any theme park or company.
